## Adobe Core 14 font metrics

`src/XRay.OneNote/Internal/Pdf/StandardFontMetrics.g.cs` holds the advance widths of the twelve
Latin PDF standard fonts (Helvetica, Times and Courier families), taken from Adobe's Core 14 AFM
font metrics files and generated by `tools/generate_standard_font_metrics.py`. Only the widths are
used; the AFM files themselves are not distributed. Adobe's terms for those files:
